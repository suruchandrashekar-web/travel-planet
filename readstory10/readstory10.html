Travel Guide

Japan is a country where ancient traditions blend beautifully with modern technology. Planning ahead helps you enjoy your trip and experience the culture with confidence.

Things to Know

🚄 Public Transport

Japan's trains are fast, clean, and punctual.

💴 Cash & Cards

Carry some cash as smaller shops may not accept cards.

🍣 Local Food

Try sushi, ramen, tempura, and regional specialties.

🏯 Respect Culture

Be polite, follow local customs, and keep public places clean.

Travel Checklist

  • Carry your passport at all times.
  • Learn a few basic Japanese phrases.
  • Purchase a transport pass if needed.
  • Pack comfortable walking shoes.
  • Check the weather before traveling.

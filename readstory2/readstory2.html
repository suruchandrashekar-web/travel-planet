Welcome to Mykonos

Mykonos is one of Greece's most famous islands, known for its beautiful beaches, whitewashed villages, blue waters and lively atmosphere. Whether you love relaxing, swimming, water sports or watching the sunset, there is a beach for everyone.

Top Beaches

Paradise Beach

A lively beach with golden sand, beach clubs and exciting nightlife.

Super Paradise Beach

Perfect for swimming, relaxing and enjoying beach parties.

Elia Beach

A peaceful beach with soft sand and crystal-clear water.

Ornos Beach

Family-friendly beach with calm water and restaurants nearby.

Psarou Beach

Luxury beach famous for upscale resorts and fine dining.

Agios Ioannis

Known for peaceful sunsets and amazing sea views.

Travel Tips

  • Carry sunscreen and sunglasses.
  • Visit beaches early during summer.
  • Bring a reusable water bottle.
  • Wear comfortable sandals.
  • Book hotels in advance.

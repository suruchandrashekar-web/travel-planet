Morocco

Morocco is a land of colorful souks, golden deserts, ancient medinas, magnificent mountains and beautiful beaches.

Why Visit Morocco?

Morocco offers unforgettable adventures—from wandering through the bustling streets of Marrakech to riding camels across the Sahara Desert. Discover historic cities, blue-painted villages, delicious cuisine, and warm hospitality.

Top Places to Visit

Marrakech

Explore colorful markets, palaces and gardens.

Chefchaouen

The famous Blue City surrounded by mountains.

Sahara Desert

Ride camels and sleep under the stars.

Travel Tips

  • ✔ Best time: March–May & September–November
  • ✔ Currency: Moroccan Dirham (MAD)
  • ✔ Famous Food: Tagine, Couscous, Mint Tea
  • ✔ Language: Arabic & French
  • ✔ Capital: Rabat

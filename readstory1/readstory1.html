California's Channel Islands

An unforgettable adventure among America's Galápagos.

By Travel Explorer | July 2026 | 8 min read

Why Visit?

California's Channel Islands National Park is home to dramatic sea cliffs, crystal-clear waters, unique wildlife, and peaceful hiking trails. Often called North America's Galápagos, these islands offer incredible outdoor experiences.

Top Things to Do

🥾 Hiking

Discover breathtaking coastal trails with panoramic ocean views.

🛶 Kayaking

Paddle through sea caves and enjoy the stunning blue waters.

🐋 Wildlife

Spot seals, dolphins, sea lions and migrating whales.

Best Time to Visit

Spring and early summer are ideal for wildflowers, pleasant weather, and whale watching.

Quick Facts

Travel Tips

  • Carry water.
  • Wear hiking shoes.
  • Book ferry tickets early.
  • Bring sunscreen.

Path of the Gods

A breathtaking hiking trail on Italy's Amalfi Coast.

Explore Guide

Trail Overview

The Path of the Gods is one of Italy's most scenic hiking trails, offering incredible views of cliffs, villages, and the Mediterranean Sea. The route is popular with hikers looking for unforgettable landscapes.

Trail Highlights

🥾 Scenic Hiking

Walk along mountain paths with panoramic coastal views.

🌊 Sea Views

Enjoy beautiful views of the Mediterranean Sea.

📸 Photo Spots

Capture unforgettable landscapes throughout the trail.

🏞 Nature

Experience fresh air, forests, and dramatic cliffs.

Trail Information

  • Distance: Approximately 7 km
  • Duration: Around 3–4 hours
  • Difficulty: Moderate
  • Best Season: Spring and Autumn
  • Wear comfortable hiking shoes.

About Appian Way

The Appian Way, also known as Via Appia Antica, is one of the world's oldest and most famous roads. Located in Rome, it offers a journey through ancient ruins, Roman history, beautiful landscapes, and archaeological treasures.

One Day Itinerary

🏛 Morning - Ancient Ruins

Start your trip by exploring Roman ruins, ancient tombs, and historic landmarks.

🚶 Afternoon - Walking Trail

Enjoy a peaceful walk or bike ride along the ancient cobblestone road.

📸 Evening - Scenic Views

Capture beautiful sunset views surrounded by Roman countryside.

Travel Tips

  • Wear comfortable walking shoes.
  • Carry water during summer.
  • Visit early to avoid crowds.
  • Use public transport or rent a bicycle.
  • Carry a camera for photography.

Hotel Selection Guide

Choosing the right hotel can make your trip more enjoyable. Consider your budget, location, room quality, guest reviews, and available facilities before making a reservation.

Things to Check Before Booking

📍 Location

Stay close to tourist attractions or public transport.

⭐ Guest Reviews

Read genuine customer reviews before booking.

💰 Budget

Compare prices across trusted booking websites.

🏊 Facilities

Check for Wi-Fi, breakfast, parking, and pool access.

Booking Tips

  • Book early for the best prices.
  • Compare hotel ratings and reviews.
  • Check cancellation policies.
  • Look for free breakfast or airport shuttle.
  • Choose hotels with high cleanliness ratings.

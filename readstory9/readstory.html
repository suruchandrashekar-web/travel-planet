About the Channel Islands

California's Channel Islands are known for their dramatic sea cliffs, beautiful beaches, unique wildlife, and peaceful hiking trails. Visitors enjoy kayaking, snorkeling, camping, whale watching, and exploring untouched nature.

Top Experiences

🥾 Hiking

Walk scenic trails with spectacular ocean views.

🛶 Kayaking

Explore sea caves and crystal-clear waters.

🐋 Whale Watching

Spot whales, dolphins, and sea lions.

🏕 Camping

Stay overnight under the stars surrounded by nature.

Travel Tips

  • Book ferry tickets early.
  • Carry enough drinking water.
  • Wear comfortable hiking shoes.
  • Bring sunscreen and a hat.
  • Pack light snacks for your trip.
